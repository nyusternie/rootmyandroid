LineageOS is the successor to CyanogenMod and the de-facto standard custom
ROM. It tracks AOSP closely, adds privacy and quality-of-life features, and
publishes per-device builds with official install guides.

Device support varies by model — check the official device wiki for your exact
model before flashing.
